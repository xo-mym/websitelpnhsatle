ANG TINIG • JOURNALISM

Ang Tinig: Ang Boses ng mga Batang Mamamahayag

Ang Tinig Club

It is a student organization that serves as a home for journalism.

To hone students' skills in writing, reporting, and research, and to foster the responsible sharing of information.

Through this club, students sharpen their critical thinking and creativity while developing an awareness of social issues, all while serving as a genuine voice for the Almanzians and the community.

Club Activities

  • Article Production: Writing news, features, editorials, science and technology, and sports columns for school publications.
  • Copyediting: Reviewing and refining drafts for grammar, style, accuracy, and fairness before publication.
  • Photojournalism: Capturing news events, campus happenings, and sports matches through visual storytelling.
  • Broadcasting: Training members in radio scriptwriting and on-air delivery for developmental and school broadcasts.
  • Press Conferences: Participating in district, regional, or national Schools Press Conferences (NSPC).
